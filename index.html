Bob's Big Run

Monster designed by the Junior Acolyte. Built by Claude in a church waiting room.

Meet Bob. Big, old, bald, and he never goes anywhere without his chainsaw.

Logs. Rev the chainsaw to chop them for +25 points, or jump right over.

Snacks. Bob's one weakness. Jump over them. Every snack he eats makes him bigger and costs a heart.

Flying snacks. They show up later, up high. Stay on the ground and chop the logs instead.

The Cake King. At 800 points a giant cake with a crown stomps in, throwing cupcakes. You can't jump him. Wait for him to charge, then chainsaw him until he's crumbs.

Big Timber. The next boss is a giant angry oak that never stops stomping toward you. Chop or jump the logs he hurls, jump the apples, and chainsaw his trunk when he gets close.

Gas can. Jump for the glowing gas can to get MEGA SAW: six seconds of non-stop golden chainsaw that even slices snacks.
